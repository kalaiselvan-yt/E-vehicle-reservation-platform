Welcome to E-Vehicle Charging Reservation Platform

As final year students, we are excited to introduce the E-Vehicle Charging Reservation Platform, a project born out of our passion for electric mobility and sustainable transportation solutions. Our goal is to create an innovative and user-friendly platform that addresses the challenges of electric vehicle charging.

Who We Are ?

We, the final year students at University college of Engineering Arni , embarked on this journey to develop the E-Vehicle Charging Reservation Platform. With our diverse backgrounds in e-Amrit and we bring fresh perspectives and enthusiasm to this project.

Our Story

The idea for the E-Vehicle Charging Reservation Platform emerged during our final year project discussions. Recognizing the growing importance of electric vehicles and the need for efficient charging infrastructure, we decided to leverage our skills to create a solution that enhances the electric mobility experience.

Our Values

  • Innovation: We embrace innovation and strive to push the boundaries of what's possible in the electric mobility industry.
  • Collaboration: We believe in the power of collaboration and teamwork to overcome challenges and achieve success.
  • Sustainability: We are committed to promoting sustainable transportation solutions for a greener future.
  • Education: We aim to educate and inspire others to embrace electric mobility through our platform.

To Whom We Serve?

The E-Vehicle Charging Reservation Platform is designed to serve electric vehicle users, enthusiasts, and stakeholders alike. Whether you're a student, professional, or business owner, our platform caters to your charging needs.

What We Offer?

  • Smart interface design for range prediction and real-time E-charge station availability.
  • User authentication and reservation management for secure and efficient charging appointments.
  • Seamless payment integration for secure transactions.
